# Rangso — Project Overview

## 1. Project Purpose

Rangso is an AI-assisted art feedback application designed to support beginner artists in developing their artistic skills.

## 2. Problem Statement

Beginner artists often struggle to identify areas for improvement in their artwork/understand how to approach those improvements.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Rangso aims to provide constructive and targeted feedback based on artistic concerns clarified by the user.

## 4. Target Audience

Beginner artists seeking guidance and support in their creative development. Primarily middle/high school students, but open to all!

## 5. Core Concept

Rather than generating artwork or trying to replace human creativity, Rangso uses technology as a tool to encourage artistic growth.

## 6. Project Goals

- Encourage independent artistic development.
- Make constructive feedback more accessible.
- Explore ethical applications of AI in art education.
- Support creative expression.
