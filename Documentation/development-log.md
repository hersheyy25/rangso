# Rangso — Development Log

This document records the development progress, research, technical decisions, challenges, and improvements made throughout Rangso's journey!

## October 2026 — Research & Project Planning

### Initial Research and Planning
- Created the basic concept for the application and decided it would be an AI assisted art feedback application.
- Recognised that there was a need for positive and easy to access feedback for beginners.
- Research into how AI can be used to help artists develop their skills.
- Basic research into how we can ethically use AI in creative industries.
- Created Notion boards to help organise my research, planning and resources.
- Planned my initial research and experiments.
- Started creating my science fair documentation using STEM Wizard.

### Project Direction
- Targeted my audience more specifically at beginner artists.
- Set my focus as helping artists develop their skills rather than replacing human creativity.
- Explored the idea of providing feedback on specific areas of concern for the artist.
